# Deliverable G: Compliance Gap Report & Remediation Action Plan

| Gap ID | Severity | Standard | Module | Description | Recommended Remediation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **GAP-01** | `High` | AAC.3.a | Medical Assessment | Initial Medical Assessment by attending physician lacks a dedicated clinical intake form with provisional diagnosis, investigation plan, and 24-hour completion validation. | Implement a structured Doctor Initial Medical Assessment form with provisional diagnosis, examination, investigation plan, and 24h timer. | Chief Medical Officer / Lead Consultant |
| **GAP-02** | `High` | AAC.7.b | Discharge Summary | No single consolidated, printable Discharge Summary document generating all AAC.7 required sections (final diagnosis, treatment summary, physical findings, post-discharge medication advice, urgent contact). | Create a consolidated Discharge Summary Generator compiling doctor orders, diagnosis, med reconciliation, and follow-up advice. | Medical Records Department & IT Head |
| **GAP-03** | `Critical` | COP.5.b | Emergency Resuscitation | Code Blue cardiac arrest resuscitation workflow lacks precise electronic response time logger (Target < 3 minutes) and CPR team log. | Add Code Blue Event Logger tracking announcement time, arrival time, response duration, CPR team leader, defibrillation shock count, and outcome (ROSC/Expired). | Emergency Medicine & Resuscitation Committee |
| **GAP-04** | `High` | COP.14.c | Operation Theatre | Pre-operative checklist exists, but intra-operative WHO 3-Phase Surgical Safety Checklist (Sign-In, Time-Out, Sign-Out) lacks dedicated time-stamped digital form. | Add intra-op Surgical Safety Checklist modal with Sign-In (before induction), Time-Out (before incision), and Sign-Out (before leaving OT). | OT Superintendent & Lead Surgeon |
| **GAP-05** | `Medium` | HIC.8.a | Biomedical Waste | Biomedical waste segregation information is disclosed to patients at admission, but hospital lacks a daily weighing and barcoded bag dispatch register in the EMR. | Add BMW Daily Register recording weights for Yellow, Red, White, and Blue categories with CBWTF barcoded manifest number. | Facility Manager & Infection Control Officer |
| **GAP-06** | `Medium` | PRE.5.a | Grievance Redressal | Patient feedback and complaints workflow currently operates through paper/verbal mechanisms without a digital grievance logging and TAT tracker in EMR. | Build a Patient Feedback & Grievance Register tracking complaint category, investigating officer, root-cause, CAPA, and closure TAT. | Patient Experience Manager & Quality Lead |
| **GAP-07** | `High` | IMS.3.b | Audit Trail | Field modifications in localStorage are saved without an immutable append-only audit trail logging user ID, workstation, and before/after values. | Implement an append-only audit log capturing every record creation, view, edit, and deletion with digital timestamp and user role. | IT Systems Architect |
